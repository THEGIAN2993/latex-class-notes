\section{Linear Transformations}
\begin{problem}
Prove that the closure of a linear subspace in a normed linear space is also a subspace.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
Prove that the operator norm defined here has the three properties required of a norm.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
Prove that the kernel of a linear functional is either closed or dense.
\end{problem}
